The Ed-Fi model,
read as an ontology.

Ed-Fi describes school data as entities, the associations that join them, and the descriptors that constrain their values. This page reads Data Standard 6.1 straight out of Ed-Fi-Core.xsd and draws what is inside it — every entity, every relationship, and every controlled vocabulary, with the Alliance's own definitions. Against 4.0 it adds 67 net entities and eight whole domains, most of them arriving at once rather than spread thin.

The one idea

Associations are things,
not just links

Ed-Fi is built from four kinds of part. Two of them are entities in their own right: a Domain Entity is a thing that exists on its own — a student, a school, a course. An Association joins two of them and, crucially, carries its own data. Enrolling a student in a school is not a foreign key. It is an object with an entry date, an exit type, a grade level, and twenty other facts of its own.

Why this matters for a crosswalk. CEDS models the same territory with Membership — a role a person holds at an organization for a period of time — and then specialises it. Ed-Fi reaches the same place from the other direction, by making each specific association a first-class entity. The two models agree far more than their shapes suggest, and that is exactly what a mapping has to express.

Worked example

Every arrow below is a real reference in the schema, with its real name. Watch step four: a grade does not hang off the student. It hangs off the association between the student and the section — because that association is the thing the grade is actually about.

Every arrow points from the entity that holds the reference to the entity it refers to, so Student Section Association → Student reads “the association names a student”. The numbered path is the reading order, not the arrow direction: start at the student, arrive at the course. Note that Grade points at the association rather than at the student — the mark belongs to that specific placement in that specific section.

The vocabularies

281 controlled vocabularies

Ed-Fi calls them descriptors. Each one is an extensible list of permitted values with a namespace URI, shipped with the standard and extended locally by each implementation. The counts below are the values the Alliance publishes with Data Standard 6.1 — a district or ISD will normally add its own on top. Ed-Fi 6 ships 91 more vocabularies than 4.0 did.

Built directly from Schemas/Bulk/Ed-Fi-Core.xsd and the 266 published descriptor files at tag v6.1.0 of Ed-Fi-Alliance-OSS/Ed-Fi-Data-Standard — the v6.0.0 and v6.0.1 core schemas are byte-identical to each other, and v6.1.0 is purely additive over them, adding 28 types and removing none, almost all of it the special-education IEP model. Names, definitions, cardinalities, and references are quoted from the schema; the grouping of entities into domains is derived from the Data Standard 6.1 interchange schemas, with a short override list for the handful of entities the interchanges do not place.
