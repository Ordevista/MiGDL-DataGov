How to read a review pack

Answers
Each respondent rated their confidence in a practice from 1 (not confident) to 4 (fully confident). "Outside my area" means the question wasn't theirs to answer. It isn't counted in any score.
Your district / region
Your respondents' answers averaged per question. A region's average counts each district once.
Confidence Index
The average answer turned into a 0–100 score: (average − 1) ÷ 3 × 100. Domain scores use the same formula over that domain's three questions.
ISD/RESA and statewide averages
The average of district scores, with each district counted once however many people responded. They appear only when enough districts have taken part that no single district can be picked out.
Spread and "apart"
Spread is the highest answer minus the lowest. "Apart" marks questions where your respondents were far apart. That often means a practice is in place in one office but not known in another, which is a good place to start a conversation.
Next steps
Your three lowest-scoring practices, with a recommended first step. Ties go to the foundation (Leadership & Strategy) first.

These are self-reported confidence ratings, not a compliance audit.
