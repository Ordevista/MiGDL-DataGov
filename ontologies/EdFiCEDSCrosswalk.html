Why the shapes differ

One spine, two constructions

The single most useful thing to understand before reading the table: CEDS has no Student, Staff, Contact, or Candidate entity. It has Person, and a person becomes a student by holding an Enrollment — one of eight specialisations of Membership. Ed-Fi does the reverse: it names the people and then makes each specific association a first-class entity.

This is why Student, Staff, Contact, and Candidate all map to Person as broad matches rather than exact ones, and why the Ed-Fi associations are the rows that hit the CEDS spine dead on. An ingestion mapping should follow the associations, not the entities — and that advice holds identically in both versions.

Within each release line the core schemas are byte-identical (v4.0.0 = v4.0.1, v6.0.0 = v6.0.1), and v6.1.0 is purely additive over v6.0, adding 28 types and removing none.

Domains are derived from each version's interchange schemas rather than assigned by hand. Candidate CEDS targets were generated by label and definition similarity, then every row was decided individually; the similarity scores are not shown because they did not decide anything. The two versions share 120 rows, and this page asserts on build that those rows carry identical decisions and identical domains in both — so every difference you see below is real model change, not drift in the mapping.
